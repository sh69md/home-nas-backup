# Privacy Policy — Home NAS Backup

Home NAS Backup is a personal backup application operated
solely by its owner.

## Information accessed

The application uses read-only Google Drive access to list
and download the owner's files and associated metadata.
Google-native documents may be exported into downloadable
file formats.

## Use and storage

Downloaded information is stored on the owner's private
home backup storage for backup and offline access.
Downloaded files and authorization credentials are not
published to GitHub or this website.

## Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data or share it
with advertising or analytics services.

## Retention and removal

Backup copies remain until the owner deletes them.
Access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account
third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops
future access but does not delete existing backup copies.

## Contact

This application is for the owner's personal use only.
The owner can be contacted through the support email
shown on the application's Google consent screen.
